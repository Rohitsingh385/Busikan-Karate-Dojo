Privacy Policy for Dojo Attendance

Last Updated: October 2026

Dojo Attendance ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users, including martial arts instructors ("Senseis"), students, and parents or legal guardians.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

A. Instructor / Administrator Accounts

  • Account credentials (email address and authentication identifiers).
  • Dojo/Academy name and instructor contact details.

B. Student Information (Provided by Instructors and Parents)

  • Full name, unique student identification code, date of birth, and assigned class/belt level.
  • Attendance history (dates present, absent, and class sessions attended).
  • Optional address and identification documents (e.g., Aadhaar copy in PDF format, collected solely with explicit parent/guardian consent for formal dojo enrollment and government sports board verification).

C. Parent & Guardian Contact Information

  • Parent/guardian full name and phone number for emergency contact and dojo communication.

2. How We Use the Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  1. Enable instructors to mark and manage class attendance records.
  2. Provide parents and students with real-time access to training schedules, attendance logs, and promotion eligibility.
  3. Facilitate essential academy notices, tournament alerts, and schedule changes.
  4. Export official attendance summaries and reports in PDF format for belt testing requirements.

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or share personal information or student data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • All application data is stored in secure, encrypted cloud databases using enterprise-grade SSL/TLS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensure that instructors only access data for students enrolled in their dojo, and parents only access records associated with their designated children.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App is designed as an administrative and parent-access tool for martial arts academies. Student records are created and managed exclusively by authorized academy administrators and parents or legal guardians. We do not solicit personal information directly from children under 13 without verifiable parental/guardian consent.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain student and attendance records for as long as the student remains actively enrolled with the martial arts academy, or until deletion is requested.

  • Account & Data Deletion: Instructors and parents can request complete deletion of their account, student profiles, and associated attendance history at any time by contacting the dojo administrator or emailing our support contact.

6. Contact Us
